## Mathematical-proofread addendum - 5 October 2026

The current review preserves the fifteen-item bibliography and does not add a novelty claim. The following distinctions govern the checks made during this pass:

- **Houston:** the primary 2016 post was read, including P4 (square subgroup) and W1 (full rational circle). The article credits the relaxation rather than claiming to originate it.
- **Brown:** the primary common-hypotenuse page and Proposition 1 page were consulted. The focused article uses the semiprime consequence and supplies its own real-angle proof. Brown's broader informal wording about the number of prime factors is not imported as a uniform theorem about arbitrary prime-power signatures.
- **SageDescent:** the official reference's covering-image bounds and rank examples were checked. This is a reference for a classical descent method, not a claim that Sage executed the finite proofs in this release.
- **Silverman--Tate:** publisher and author metadata confirm the second edition, 2015 date, authors, DOI, and relevant chapter topics. The complete book was not retrieved. No invented page or theorem locator is supplied.
- **Bremner:** the primary IMPAN publication record confirms Acta Arithmetica 88(3), 289--297 (1999), DOI 10.4064/aa-88-3-289-297. The full paper was not used as a proof premise.
- **Robertson:** primary publisher metadata confirm Mathematics Magazine 69(4), 289--293 (1996). The website's 2018 online-publication date is not substituted for the 1996 issue year. Full text was not obtained here; the citation remains contextual.
- **BTV:** the author institution's repository confirms title, authors, journal, volume/issue/pages and DOI. Its abstract explicitly identifies the magic-square surface among the applications. No global finiteness theorem from this work is asserted by the focused paper.
- **Weisenberg:** the journal abstract explicitly proposes computation by the prime signature of the center. This is the exact limited claim cited; the complete article was not reread here.
- **Cain, Wolird, Auel--Singer:** arXiv version metadata and the relevant abstract/context were checked. These are contextual references, not external premises for either principal theorem.
- **SymPy:** the official software-citation record and pinned version are preserved; a software citation is not a mathematical authority for a successful computation.
- **Public prime-support archive:** the earlier pinned valuation attribution is retained. The current two-block theorem statement was consulted only to confirm the difference in hypotheses. Its proof is not assumed, and no claim of independent priority follows from this review.
