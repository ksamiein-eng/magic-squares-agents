## Computational routes

Two-prime producer/certificates: `verification/two_prime/`.
The standard-library checker reconstructs equations by sine addition; the symbolic checker uses Chebyshev polynomials and direct Sylvester determinants. Exact evaluation on D+1 inputs is accompanied by an independent degree bound D; it is not a numerical sampling argument.

D1 catalog/descent/reconstruction: `verification/d1_norm/`.
The two normalization constraints are both retained. The primitive-residue charts cover every denominator choice. The symbolic transfer checker relates the catalog to the original ordered source matrices.

Article-only checks: `check_paper.py`.
This checks the explicit curve polynomial and factorization, squarefreeness and known fibers, conic and circle identities, reconstruction, rational nondegeneracy polynomials, matrix maps, finite orbit coverage, displayed certificate totals, citation keys, and LaTeX cross-reference keys. It is not a substitute for running the main verifiers.

The theorem proofs cite standard descent and torsion-reduction results. The selected main claims do not use the broader record's published positive-rank basis premise. Peer review and historical novelty are not machine-checked properties.
