# Editorial decisions and submission boundary

## What was changed

The 43-page development manuscript has been rewritten as a focused article centered on two main theorems and their structural connection. The opening states the exact rational-circle domain, the sum-seven exclusion, and the reversible genus-five reduction. Chronological phrases such as "this round", counts "beyond the original audit", and claims of novelty based on repeated AI checking have been removed from the paper.

The arithmetic dictionary, Gaussian angle, primitivity bridge, complete signed assignments, projective boundary treatment, and explicit reconstruction remain in the text. The D1 proof includes the two simultaneous square conditions, the complete norm catalog, the three rank-zero elliptic groups, actual finite residue witnesses, the conic parametrization, and every rational nondegeneracy exception. The torus section prints the signed matrix identities and distinguishes unimodular equivalences from one-way maps of degree two.

The main article does not need the positive-rank full-basis database matches, canonical-height computations, large nonlinear normalization inventory, or the older five-orbit closure theorem. They remain in the extended record and proof trees. Omitting them is a scope decision, not a retraction and not an implicit certification of a larger theorem.

## Citation and contribution corrections

The most important added source is Robin Houston's 2016 P4/W1 post. The broad rational-circle relaxation must be attributed, not claimed as an invention of this project. The contribution statement now concerns the particular exclusions, explicit transfer maps, and exact normalization-based reduction.

Brown receives credit for the triangle formulation and semiprime arithmetic obstruction. Weisenberg is credited for center-prime-signature organization. Cain and Wolird are credited for Gaussian approaches. The overlapping public project is cited by its project attribution and pinned source, without assuming its stronger arithmetic claims. Global surface geometry is distinguished from the specific rational-circle subfamilies studied here.

Source verification is bounded. In particular, the full papers of Robertson, Bremner, and Weisenberg were not all retrieved during this pass. Their citations support the limited contextual statements and metadata documented in CITATION_AUDIT.md; they are not represented as a complete theorem-by-theorem originality comparison. Failure to locate the exact genus-five polynomial in a search is not evidence of priority.

## What has actually been rerun

The preceding 24-stage research suite passed before the rewrite. The focused release additionally has an eight-stage proof suite and article-specific exact identity, table, and reference checks. The source and the compiled PDF use the same theorem scope. Execution records are preserved under verification_runs/.

No new mathematical exclusion is asserted merely because the paper is reorganized. The eight-point list on the genus-five curve remains a conjecture. No human referee or independent mathematician reviewed this work as part of this turn. Separate programs reduce implementation risk but do not establish human-independent mathematical validation.

## Author decisions still required before submission

1. Supply the real human author names, affiliations or independent-researcher designation, and contact information. Do not add an AI system as an author.
2. Read and defend each central proof and its dependence on the executable certificates. Obtain a competent independent mathematical reading if possible; that reader need not be a coauthor.
3. Complete a focused comparison of the main statements with the closest full texts, including equivalent curve models and the other project's arithmetic hypotheses. The present draft does not claim first-in-literature priority.
4. Deposit the exact code/certificate release at a stable public address, choose its license, and insert the real persistent reference. The companion archive is currently supplied with the manuscript, but has no public DOI.
5. Check the target journal's scope, source-file rules, and AI-disclosure policy. The current statement accurately reports extensive assistance with mathematics, code, checking, and prose; it must not be weakened to a false grammar-only description.

These are submission-preparation and responsibility tasks. A complete classification of the ten remaining whole orbits is not a prerequisite for submitting the correctly scoped results in this article.
