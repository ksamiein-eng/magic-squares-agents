The problem: nine distinct squares in a 3×3 magic square?

Centre entry y². Each line through the centre is a Pythagorean triangle on hypotenuse y ⇒ a point of the rational unit circle T(Q).

Magic ⇔ four sines of integer combinations of the angles have magnitudes {a, b, a+b, a−b}.

Rigidity conjecture: for every full-depth frequency matrix this never happens on T(Q)^r. Strictly stronger than "no magic square of squares".

Proved in the paper

  • r = 2, exponent sum ≤ 7: 2,064 exact certificates
  • D1 ⇔ extra rational point on a genus-5 curve
  • r = 4: nothing at projective height ≤ 4 (11,862 configurations)
  • 48 Hadamard surfaces are of general type

Open: the eight-point list (Conj 7.3), sum ≥ 8, the torsion-coset hypothesis (Conj 8.6), ten orbits.

Step 2 — a team, not a pipeline

The loop: convert → ledger → attack → referee → absorb → iterate

Ledger. Every open target typed: certificate-extension, bounded-search, hypothesis-probe, structural. State, acceptance, best tag, next increment.

Playbooks. Paper-agnostic strategies with acceptance criteria and failure modes. Type → playbook → agent.

Planner. propose_next_attack(P1) → "exponent sum 9, this command, this cost, this stop rule".

Referee contract. tag_lint.py fails the build if any claim lacks a tag, an evidence file, a verifier, or a passing stage.

Absorb. Accepted results become stages 17+ of the paper's own verification suite, run by the paper's own runner. The paper stays byte-identical.

Iterate. Iteration 2 (sum 9, height 4) was launched by the planner's own commands before the deadline.
