The problem

Nine distinct squares in a 3×3 magic square. Open for decades.

Centre y². Each line through it is a Pythagorean triangle on hypotenuse y, so a point of the rational unit circle.

Magic ⇔ four sines of integer combinations of the angles have magnitudes {a, b, a+b, a−b}.

Rigidity conjecture: this never happens on the rational circle, for any full-depth frequency matrix. Strictly stronger than the original problem.

What the paper proved before today

  • r = 2, exponent sum ≤ 7: 2,064 exact certificates
  • first three-parameter case ⇔ a genus-5 curve has only 8 rational points
  • r = 4: nothing at height ≤ 4; Hadamard surfaces of general type

Open this morning

  • the eight-point list · sums ≥ 8 · the torsion-coset hypothesis · ten orbits

Convert → ledger → attack → referee → absorb → iterate

Iteration 1 · finding 1

The curve had two symmetries nobody used

C: Y² = h¹² + 70h¹⁰ − 1009h⁸ + 7060h⁶ − 1009h⁴ + 70h² + 1 is even and palindromic ⇒ h → −h and h → 1/h.

Its Jacobian splits off three elliptic curves. The paper's own known points give points of infinite order on each (exact Nagell–Lutz). PROVED

The failure that became a theorem

Iteration 1 guessed. Iteration 2 refuted it. The refutation explained itself.

H ≤ 3

exponent rank never above 2. Recorded as a candidate sharpening.

H = 4

13,824 rank-3 points in orbits 0 and 54. Sharpening refuted.

Lemma

a zero sine is itself a relation Ar; equal magnitudes give Ai ± Aj. Rank 3 needs Ai ± Aj ∥ Ar. PROVED

23/113

orbits satisfy the criterion; the two observed are among them; at height 5 still exactly those two.

Trichotomy. On a signed system the magnitudes are forced to be {A, B, A+B, A−B}: a rational solution is admissible, has a vanishing sine, or has A = 2B. All 1,916,672 points have a vanishing sine; none has A = 2B. PROVED SEARCH-EVIDENCE

Vanishing-sine rigidity (new conjecture, strictly stronger than the paper's) implies both open conjectures and names the next search. CONJECTURAL

Why now

Candidate theorems are cheap. Verified status is not.

openai/math · October 2026

719 AI-generated manuscripts on 372 open problems. About 42% formalized in Lean. The rest "could have issues". Three withdrawn within a day.

this loop

every claim tagged by a fresh referee · one command reproduces all six agent stages · verbatim transcripts with hashes · corrections recorded, including its own · formalization status stated per result (0% today, candidates with exact statements)

The bottleneck moved from producing theorems to making each claim's status explicit, checkable and correctable. That is what this loop is.

Beyond magic squares

What the loop needs, and where it goes next

Needs: code or certificates to bind tools to · open targets that can be typed · a verification suite to absorb into.

Did not do: close a conjecture; run on a second paper.

Next instances: the paper's other six targets (planner already emits them) · quadratic Chabauty on the genus-2 quotient · any "proved for N ≤ N₀ by certificate" paper · any curve with a conjecturally complete point list · any "every solution has property X" hypothesis.
