## Three most serious issues

1. The count "935,552 points recorded at H<=4" (rigidity.tex after the trichotomy, CHANGES_v3 row 5, RESULTS.md Finding 6) adds the H=3 and H=4 totals, but the H=3 grid is a subset of the H=4 grid and the probe is the same, so the H=3 points are re-found at H=4. The defensible number is 662,528 (the abstract already uses it). Nothing else depends on the figure.
2. The sum-9 certificate rests on the standard-library checker alone (strict PASS, reproduced by me), without the SymPy crosscheck and mutation tests that sum 8 had. The theorem's proof says so honestly; the abstract's "independently re-checked" is the only place that could be read as the full iteration-1 protocol. The verifier diff being constants-only is a real mitigation and should be stated where the caveat is.
3. Wording drift around the new conjecture: RESULTS.md says the problem "collapses to one statement" and the abstract says the data "support the single hypothesis". Vanishing-sine rigidity implies Conjectures 8.1 and 8.6 but is strictly stronger than 8.1 (it also excludes type (iii)); the data are consistent with it because no type-(iii) point has been seen, which at H<=4 with grid primes 5, 13, 17 is weak evidence. Also "the remaining 21 orbits are a prediction" (rigidity.tex) should be "candidates": the parallel-triple criterion is necessary, not sufficient.

## What moved

Since the first pass, one theorem of the relaxed problem genuinely advanced: the certified exclusion now covers k+l<=9 (strict checker re-run and PASS here), still with the caveat that its arithmetic corollary is subsumed by the archive's two-prime theorem. Two small pieces of mathematics are correct and new: the degeneracy-relations lemma (which explains why rank-3 points exist at H=4 and bounds degenerate points at rank 3) and the trichotomy on a signed system, which reduces rigidity plus torsion-coset to a single clean hypothesis and identifies the one untested failure mode (A=2B with no vanishing sine). All data at H<=4 are consistent with that hypothesis, but the probe's grid carries only three split primes, so this is early evidence. The unfilled placeholders and empty section I flagged in pass one are gone; the quotient-section tags are now correct. No conjecture of the paper changed status.
