## Three most serious issues

1. Point-hunter REPORT.md is unfinished: `C_BOUND`, `D_BOUND`, `EXTENDED_RUNS_PLACEHOLDER` remain in the headline result and the "what this proves" section. The numbers exist in `search_results.json` (300,000 / 100,000 / 2,000); the text must be filled before anything is cited.
2. Certificate-extender REPORT.md ends with an empty section "Status of the secondary checks". The evidence shows why: `crosscheck_degree8_sympy.log` contains 44 PASS lines, only one of which (`44_m1_n1`) is a sum-8 case, and `test_degree8.log` is 0 bytes. The main claim does not depend on these, but the report must say explicitly that the SymPy crosscheck covered 1/32 new cases and the mutation tests did not run.
3. `genus5_quotient_structure.json` mixes proved and quoted statements in one "conclusion" sentence: "rank = 1" (LMFDB) is used to get "rank Jac(D) = 2 = g(D), classical Chabauty fails". That is a DATABASE-QUOTED conclusion, and the independence argument for "rank Jac(C) >= 3" is not written down (it holds, see table). Tag both.

## What moved

Nothing changed the logical status of any theorem in the paper. The k+l<=8 certificate is a real, independently re-verified extension of Theorem 1.1 for the relaxed rational-circle problem (its arithmetic corollary was already covered by the two-prime theorem). The bounded searches (C to 300k, D to 100k, torsion cosets to H=3) are all consistent with Conjectures 7.3 and 8.6 but prove neither. The one structurally useful new fact is the quotient analysis: all three elliptic quotients provably have positive rank, so the paper's rank-zero descent route to Conjecture 7.3 is closed, and (modulo LMFDB ranks) classical Chabauty on D is also closed; quadratic Chabauty on D or elliptic-curve Chabauty remain the live routes.
